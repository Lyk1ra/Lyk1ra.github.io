About

Profile

I am an undergraduate student in the School of Integrated Circuits at Nanjing University (2023–2027), majoring in Integrated Circuit Design and Integrated Systems. My undergraduate research interests are AI efficiency and, in particular, quantization.

In 2027, I will join Shanghai Jiao Tong University as a PhD student in Intelligent Science and Technology. I will be advised by Professor Shaobo Cui and plan to focus on world models.

Research and Projects

  • LiftQuant: Continuous Bit-Width Control for Pareto-Optimal LLM Deployment (ICML 2026).
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting scene reconstruction using drone imagery.
  • FPGA-based Gzip compressor.

Honor
